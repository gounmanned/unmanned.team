The journey

How we take over your security in 6 steps

From your first invitation email to a fully monitored, actively defended business — here's what happens and when.

Accept your invitation

We'll send you an email invitation to get started. Once you click to accept it, we're able to start managing your account.

We connect your monitors

We'll work with you to set up monitors — integrations that plug into your identity platform (Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace), your registrar (usually Cloudflare), and any other services you rely on, like HubSpot. The more you connect, the more visibility Vex has.

These monitors work around the clock, analyzing security events and alerts and sending them straight into our 24/7 manned SOC.

We deploy endpoint agents

Next, we deploy lightweight agents on your Windows, Mac, and Linux computers.

On Windows and Mac, these agents apply patches automatically. Across all three, they send event logs and security alerts straight to the SOC.

Our SOC takes it from here

Our SOC monitors your account day and night, applying proactive fixes to your environment — always tuning — and responding directly to any cyber attack.

If something event-worthy is happening, a SOC analyst will contact you directly. You won't need to go looking for trouble.

Get your monthly rundown

Every month, you'll get an email with a rundown of your account — what was prevented, detected, and responded to, in plain language. Oh, and from a real human.

Run a pen test on demand

Vex is already running red team techniques against your environment continuously, so when you need a formal pen test for a compliance requirement like SOC 2, we can produce one on demand.
