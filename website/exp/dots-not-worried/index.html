What does a Dot do?

Dots make your computer hard to hack.

A small program that looks after your computer by itself. It finds the openings attackers use to get in, like an app that missed an update, and closes what it can before anyone tries them. No jargon and nothing to babysit.

  1. Tests your defenses

    Your Dot runs a safe, controlled test to see if your protection actually stops it, not just whether it’s turned on.

  2. Fixes what’s safe to fix

    Missing updates and risky settings get fixed on their own.

  3. Asks before bigger changes

    Anything that could change how you use your computer waits for your OK.

  4. Tells you what it can’t fix

    You get a plain-English note and a real person to help.

Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your Dot only looks at how your computer is set up, like updates and security settings. It isn’t there to read your files, photos or messages. We never sell your information.

Will it slow down my computer?

Dots are tiny by design and built to stay out of your way. Most people never notice it’s there.

Do I still need antivirus?

Keep it. Your Dot runs alongside your antivirus and even tests that it’s doing its job.

How do I install it?

After you sign up, we’ll email you the instructions. Dots work on Mac and Windows and take only a couple of minutes to install.

What’s in the monthly report?

A short, plain-English summary of what your Dot tested, what it fixed and anything that needs you.

Can I uninstall it myself?

Yes, anytime. Remove it like any other app, or send us a message and we’ll walk you through it.

I have more than one computer. How does that work?

Each computer gets its own Dot, at 99¢ a month each, billed yearly.

We’d love to. For more on how Dots work, read our research (opens in a new tab). Then let us know what you have in mind.
