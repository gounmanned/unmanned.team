Who it's for

Anyone who handles security operations and already spends the day in Slack.

  • SOC AnalystTriages incoming alerts
  • Security EngineerOwns tooling and integrations
  • Detection & Response EngineerTunes detections, runs playbooks
  • Incident ResponderHandles confirmed incidents
  • Security Operations ManagerRuns the team and its coverage
  • CISO / Head of SecurityNeeds visibility, not another console
  • IT AdministratorManages Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace
  • MSP TechnicianOnboards client tenants

How it works

From install to connected, every step after the first click happens inside Slack.

  1. 1
    Step 1

    Add VEX to Slack

    Click Add to Slack and approve the install. Slack asks which channel VEX should post to; pick the one your security team already watches.

    VEX is requesting permission to access the Acme Security workspace
    Where should VEX post?
    # security-ops
    CancelAllow
  2. 2
    Step 2

    Get your credentials

    Follow the guide for your provider to create the credentials VEX uses. You'll paste them into Slack in the next step.

    Microsoft 365 setupvex.unmanned.team/docs/microsoft Google Workspace setupvex.unmanned.team/docs/google
  3. 3
    Step 3

    Connect with /connect

    In the channel you chose, run the command for your provider with your credentials. VEX replies in the channel when the connection is live.

    /connect microsoft <credentials>
    /connect google <credentials>
    Commands matching "/connect"
    /connect microsoft <credentials>VEX · Connect Microsoft 365
    /connect google <credentials>VEX · Connect Google Workspace
    /connect microsoft eyJ0ZW5hbnQiOi…
  4. 4
    Step 4

    Disconnect anytime

    Switching providers or offboarding a tenant takes one command.

    /disconnect microsoft
    /disconnect google
    Marcus 4:12 PM

  5. You're set up

    VEX posts to your channel from here on.
