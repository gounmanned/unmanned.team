Meet your Dot

What does a Dot actually do?

It’s a little peace of mind, quietly keeping cyber-badness out.

A Dot is a small program that looks after your computer by itself. It checks for the openings attackers use to get in, like an app that missed an update. Your Dot finds them first and fixes them. No settings, no jargon and nothing to babysit.

  1. Tiny and light

    Your Dot is built to be small. It runs quietly in the background, so you can get on with your day.

  2. Looks for weak spots

    It checks for the things attackers look for, like missing updates or phishing emails.

  3. Fixes what it finds

    Your Dot patches what it can, so the door is closed before anyone tries it.

  4. Then stays out of your way

    Back to your photos, email and bills. Your Dot keeps watch in the background.

Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Can my Dot see my personal files?

Your Dot looks at how your computer is set up, like updates and security settings. It isn’t there to read your files, photos or messages.

Will it slow down my computer?

Dots are tiny by design and built to stay out of your way. Most people never notice it’s there.

Do I still need antivirus?

Keep it. Your Dot runs alongside your antivirus and covers different ground: closing the gaps attackers use to get in.

How do I install it?

After you sign up, follow our step-by-step install guide. It works on Mac and Windows and takes a couple of minutes.

What’s in the monthly report?

A short summary of what your Dot checked and what it fixed, in plain English. It’s easy to understand and simple to follow.

Can I uninstall it myself?

Yes, anytime. Remove it like any other app, or send us a message and we’ll walk you through it.

What happens if I cancel?

Your Dot removes itself from your computer. There’s nothing left to clean up.

I have more than one computer. How does that work?

Each computer gets its own Dot, at 99¢ a month each.
