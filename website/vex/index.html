Prevent

An LLM hacker looks for ways in

This year, researchers watched autonomous AI systems run real intrusions against government networks with no human at the keyboard — mapping infrastructure and cracking accounts almost entirely on their own. That's no longer a hypothetical for the red team side either. Vex runs that same class of AI attacker against your business, deliberately and around the clock, so the paths it finds get closed before someone with worse intentions finds them first.

Detect

It alerts when it's not alone

An environment under real attack rarely stays quiet for long. Vex's AI hacker is already inside your systems, watching for the same signs a second intruder would leave — new logins, unfamiliar processes, activity that doesn't match how your team actually works — and it raises the alarm to your SOC the moment something else is moving where it shouldn't be.

Respond

It responds in milliseconds

Once a breach looks likely, minutes matter more than anything else. Vex doesn't wait on a human to review the alert first — it can lock the account, isolate the device, or cut off the asset in milliseconds, then hand your SOC an incident that's already contained instead of one that's still spreading.
