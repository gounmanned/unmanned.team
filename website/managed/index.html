The journey

What to expect, from day one

From your first invitation email to a fully monitored, actively defended business — here's exactly what happens and when. You won't need a security team of your own, and you won't need to watch a dashboard.

Accept your invitation

We'll send you an email invitation to get started. Once you click to accept it, we're able to start managing your account.

Connect your monitors

We'll work with you to set up monitors — integrations that plug into your identity platform (Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace), your registrar (usually Cloudflare), and any other services you rely on, like HubSpot. The more you connect, the more visibility Vex has.

Install endpoint agents

Next, we deploy lightweight agents across all employee computers. These agents apply patches automatically and send event logs and security alerts straight to the SOC.

We take it from here

We monitor your account day and night, applying proactive adjustments — always tuning — and responding directly to any cyberattack.

Get your monthly rundown

Every month, you'll get an email with a rundown of your account — what was prevented, detected, and responded to, in plain language. Oh, and from a real human.
