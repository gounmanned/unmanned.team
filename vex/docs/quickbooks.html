QuickBooks

This guide walks you through adding a QuickBooks monitor in Vex. The monitor does not require any credentials or API keys. You can connect it instantly to begin tracking Intuit QuickBooks service status and outages.

Step 1: Open Monitors in Vex

  1. Log into your Vex dashboard.
  2. Navigate to the Monitors section from the sidebar.

Step 2: Add QuickBooks Monitor

  1. Click Add Monitor.
  2. Select QuickBooks from the list of available integrations.
  3. Enter {} in the configuration box.
  4. Click Connect.

What Is Monitored

The QuickBooks monitor continuously checks Intuit’s service health to detect outages and disruptions.

  • QuickBooks Online service availability
  • API and integration outages
  • Login and authentication disruptions
  • Payment and sync service degradation
  • General Intuit platform incident status

How Alerts Work

If an outage or degradation is detected, Vex will automatically notify you so you can respond quickly to service disruptions affecting QuickBooks.
