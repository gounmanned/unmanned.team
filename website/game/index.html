REC · 00:00:01 ORIGIN
FIELD NOTE — ORIGIN ON RECORD

This game didn't start with Vex. It started in an auditorium.

For years, our founder David Hunt ran an annual exercise against U.S. Army cyber defenders using a top-secret attack platform he built — against a realistic 2,500-endpoint range wired into a live SIEM.

100 to 150 defenders would sit together and watch their tools. David's team ran exactly one attack, on one endpoint. When a defender found where it happened and what it was, they rang a bell.

The point was never the score. It was tuning — the tech, and the humans behind it — for the moment that actually matters: the one real attacker who gets in.
