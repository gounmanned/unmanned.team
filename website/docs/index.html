About this guide

Who this is for

This guide is for MSP and MSSP partners who've purchased a Vex license and are setting up for the first time. It walks through registration, connecting your first monitors, and the day-to-day workflow of triaging signals, managing assets, and reviewing threats across one or many customer accounts.

The journey

Get to know Vex in 10 steps

Vex is a complete Managed Detection & Response platform that thinks like a hacker but triages like an AI SOC.

Register an account

Email go@unmanned.team to request a free account. Once Unmanned enables it, log in at vex.unmanned.team.

Accounts are partitioned by domain name, so any people that share the same email domain will automatically group together.

Wait, but how much does this cost? We charge a flat (small) fee for each customer account you manage inside the platform. But we let you pilot the platform free of charge, for as long as you need.

Familiarize yourself with the dashboard

The dashboard works like a ticketing system. Every security weakness Vex finds is logged as a signal.

New signals appear as you connect monitors, and independently from Vex's own ongoing public exposure scraping.

Connect monitors

Start with an identity monitor — Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 — to pull in user accounts and assets, and a Cloudflare monitor to pull in domains and DNS records.

Add an RMM or EDR monitor next. We recommend Level.io, if you don't have one (it's great). Endpoint monitors ship security alerts and event logs into Vex.

Finally, add a notification monitor like Slack or Jira, so all signals stream to you in real-time!

Triage tickets

By now, you probably see some tickets. As monitors run, signals land on the board. Click into one to see full context, Vex's automated response, and its recommended resolution.

Each signal carries a status — active, blocked, acceptable risk, false positive, resolved, or auto-resolved by Vex — and you can leave commentary directly on the ticket.

Vex will attempt to automatically triage signals wherever possible, otherwise leaving them open for a human to take a look.

Manage assets

Open asset inventory to see every domain and user identity Vex is tracking.

Mark an asset as priority with the star icon, or suspend it with the pause icon. Suspending revokes access tokens and disables that person or domain immediately.

Review threat actors

Vex analyzes every attack against your account and correlates activity across all connected monitors.

When the same attacker shows up in more than one place, Vex groups that activity into a threat actor you can review and determine if you need to take action.

Check today's breach scenario

Breach Scenarios shows "the breach of the day" — Vex's read on all open signals and the most probable path an attacker would take through your business right now.

It's built to show what an adversarial AI would do if let loose in your environment, and it highlights a single choke point: the one signal every attack path runs through. Fix that first.

Confirm file backups

Every day, Vex takes differential backups — files that changed in the last 24 hours — across Microsoft and Google ecosystems. Backups run at 5pm EST.

Invite your first customer

Click "Switch tenant" to move into the MSP view, where you manage every customer account from one place.

Invite your first customer from the left sidebar — they'll receive an email invitation to accept. Once accepted, you can delegate into their account and invite additional admins beyond yourself.

White label the user interface

Clone the web interface at https://github.com/gounmanned/unmanned.team and follow the steps to white label the UI. You can apply your own branding, colors, logos and adjust any part of the experience. When you're done, you can run it locally or deploy it on your own infrastructure.

Or, if you prefer to stay out of the UI, check out the API.
