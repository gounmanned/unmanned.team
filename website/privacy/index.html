Unmanned Privacy Policy

Unmanned, Inc. · Effective July 7, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Unmanned, Inc. ("Unmanned," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with VEX, including the VEX application at vex.unmanned.team, the VEX Slack app, endpoint agents, APIs, and our websites (collectively, the "Service").

This policy covers information we collect when you visit our websites, create an account, communicate with us, or use the Service.

When a business uses VEX, that business (the "Customer") generally controls the information it connects to the Service, and Unmanned processes that information on the Customer's behalf. In those circumstances, the Customer is responsible for determining the purposes and means of processing, and its privacy policies and instructions may also apply. Our processing of Customer Data is governed by the applicable customer agreement and data processing addendum ("DPA"). Where those agreements conflict with this policy, they control with respect to Customer Data.

2. Information we collect

Account information

We collect information associated with accounts and authorized users, including names, email addresses, authentication information, and account configuration details.

Customer-connected data

When a Customer connects systems such as Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, GitHub, AWS, Cloudflare, Slack, or other supported integrations, VEX accesses information through those systems' APIs to provide security monitoring, analysis, and testing. Depending on the integrations and features enabled, this may include:

  • Identity records, including user, service, and AI-agent accounts and associated email addresses.
  • Domains, DNS records, and other attack-surface information.
  • Security alerts, authentication events, audit logs, and configuration settings.
  • Asset inventories, vulnerabilities, security findings, and assessment results.
  • Information generated through authorized security testing and defensive operations.

VEX accesses information according to the permissions and scopes granted by the Customer and the features enabled. We encourage Customers to grant only the permissions necessary for their intended use.

Integration credentials

We collect and process API keys, OAuth tokens, service-account credentials, and other authentication information provided to connect Customer systems to VEX.

Endpoint agent data

If a Customer installs a VEX endpoint agent, the agent collects device and security telemetry necessary to provide its enabled functionality. This may include device identifiers, operating system and patch information, installed software, configuration details, and security events.

The agent is designed to collect security-related device and application telemetry rather than the contents of personal files, messages, or general browsing activity. The specific information collected depends on the agent's features and configuration.

Billing information

Payments are processed by Stripe or other designated payment providers. We may receive billing contact information, subscription details, and transaction records. We do not receive or store full payment card numbers when payments are processed directly by our payment provider.

Usage and technical information

We may collect IP addresses, browser and device information, timestamps, access records, and actions performed within the Service. We use this information to operate the Service, troubleshoot issues, maintain security, and investigate potential misuse.

Communications

We collect information you provide when contacting us, requesting support, submitting feedback, or communicating with Unmanned.

3. How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and support the Service.
  • Perform security monitoring, testing, analysis, and authorized response activities.
  • Identify attack paths, security risks, and vulnerabilities and generate findings and recommendations.
  • Secure our systems, prevent abuse, and maintain audit records.
  • Manage accounts, subscriptions, billing, and customer communications.
  • Improve the reliability, performance, and functionality of the Service.
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information, share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use Customer Data to train general-purpose AI models for other customers.

We may use aggregated or de-identified information to analyze usage, improve the Service, and develop new capabilities, provided the information is not reasonably capable of identifying a Customer or individual.

4. Artificial intelligence

VEX uses artificial intelligence and automated systems to analyze security information, identify threats, conduct authorized security testing, and generate findings, recommendations, and responses.

Information processed by these systems may include Customer Data, security events, identity information, asset inventories, and testing results.

We may use third-party AI service providers to support these functions. Such providers process information on our behalf and are subject to applicable contractual confidentiality and data protection obligations. We do not authorize these providers to use Customer Data submitted through VEX to train general-purpose AI models for other customers.

AI-generated findings and recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate. Automated operations are performed within the permissions and scope configured or authorized by the Customer.

5. Authorized security operations

VEX may perform security monitoring, automated assessments, adversarial simulations, and defensive actions against systems and identities that a Customer has connected and authorized for those purposes.

These activities may generate network traffic, authentication events, security alerts, configuration changes, or other activity within connected systems.

Customers are responsible for ensuring they have the necessary authority to grant access and authorize testing or other operations. Security findings and assessment results are made available to the Customer that requested them and are treated as confidential Customer information.

6. Sharing and subprocessors

We disclose information only as reasonably necessary to provide and operate the Service, comply with legal obligations, or as otherwise described in this policy.

  • Service providers and subprocessors: Providers that support hosting, infrastructure, payment processing, AI functionality, communications, monitoring, and other operational services. They may process information on our behalf under applicable contractual protections.
  • Integrations: Third-party systems and services that Customers choose to connect, including destinations to which VEX sends alerts or other authorized information.
  • Legal requirements: Government authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, or valid legal process. Where legally permitted, we will provide notice to the affected Customer.
  • Business transfers: Parties involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection protections.

7. Third-party services

The Service integrates with third-party platforms and products that are independently operated and governed by their own privacy policies and terms. When a Customer connects a third-party service, VEX accesses information according to the permissions granted by that Customer.

Unmanned does not control the privacy practices or data handling of third-party providers. Customers should review the applicable terms and privacy policies of the services they connect.

8. Security

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. These safeguards include, as applicable:

  • Encryption in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encryption at rest using AES-256.
  • Encryption of integration credentials and restricted access to stored secrets.
  • Least-privilege access controls, role-based permissions, and multi-factor authentication.
  • Logical separation of Customer data.
  • Logging and monitoring of access to production systems.
  • Security testing and monitoring of our infrastructure.

Access to Customer information is restricted to authorized personnel and service providers with a legitimate operational need. Authorized Unmanned personnel may access Customer information when reasonably necessary to provide support, investigate security incidents, maintain the Service, or fulfill contractual obligations.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to maintain safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information we process.

Customers may request additional information about our security practices and architecture through their Unmanned contact.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill contractual obligations, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise applicable rights, contact us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity before responding and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

If your information was provided to VEX by a Customer, such as your employer, that Customer generally determines how the information is used. In those circumstances, please contact the Customer first. We will assist the Customer in responding to applicable requests.

Customers control which integrations VEX can access and may disconnect integrations through the available controls or by contacting us.

Where applicable, you may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. Cookies and website technologies

Our websites and online services may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, maintain sessions, understand website usage, and improve performance.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where required by law, we will request consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

12. International transfers

Unmanned is based in the United States, and information processed through the Service may be stored or accessed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we use appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law, which may include Standard Contractual Clauses or other recognized transfer mechanisms.

13. Children

The Service is intended for business and professional use and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take appropriate steps to delete it, subject to applicable law.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, operations, or legal obligations. We will update the effective date when changes are made. For material changes, we will provide notice to Customers by email or through the Service where reasonably practicable.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
