Why this matters

Attackers look for an open door

Before anyone tries anything sophisticated, they do what any operative does first: walk the perimeter and test every door. The unlocked one wins. In your business, that's a forgotten ex-employee account, an email system with no spam filtering, or a domain anyone can impersonate.

Most breaches aren't clever. They're opportunistic — someone found something obvious that nobody had closed. Often something that takes ten minutes to fix, once you know it's there.

Most business owners have never seen this view of their own company. Not because it's hidden — it's public information, sitting in DNS records and login pages anyone can reach — but because nobody's ever pointed a flashlight at it and told them what they were looking at.

The goal isn't to be bulletproof. Just close the doors.
