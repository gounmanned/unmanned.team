Unmanned Data Processing Addendum

Unmanned, Inc. · Effective November 9, 2026

1. Agreement

This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of the Terms of Service or other agreement governing your use of VEX (the "Agreement") between you and Unmanned, Inc. ("Unmanned," "we," or "us"). Capitalized terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Agreement, including "Service," "Customer Data," and "Confidential Information."

This DPA applies when Unmanned processes Personal Data contained in Customer Data on your behalf in providing the Service. By accepting the Agreement, you agree to this DPA on behalf of yourself and, where applicable, your organization and any affiliates permitted to use the Service under your account.

If there is a conflict, the following order of precedence applies: (a) the Standard Contractual Clauses, where they apply; (b) this DPA; and (c) the Agreement. If you have a separately signed data processing agreement with Unmanned, that agreement controls to the extent of any conflict with this DPA.

2. Definitions

"Data Protection Laws" means all laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679 ("GDPR"), the GDPR as incorporated into UK law and the UK Data Protection Act 2018 ("UK GDPR"), the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection ("FADP"), and U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended ("CCPA").

"Personal Data" means any information in Customer Data that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person, or that is otherwise defined as "personal data," "personal information," or a similar term under Data Protection Laws.

"Security Incident" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to Personal Data processed by Unmanned or its Subprocessors. Security alerts, authentication events, or other activity generated by authorized testing or automated response under Section 3 of the Terms are not Security Incidents.

"Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs" means the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission in Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914.

"Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by Unmanned to process Personal Data on your behalf.

"Controller," "processor," "data subject," "processing," "service provider," and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in Data Protection Laws.

3. Roles and scope

As between the parties, you are the controller (or a processor acting on behalf of your own controller), and Unmanned is your processor (or subprocessor) with respect to Personal Data in Customer Data. Unmanned is a controller only of account, billing, and business contact information it processes as described in our Privacy Policy, which is outside the scope of this DPA.

The subject matter, nature, purpose, and duration of processing, the categories of Personal Data, and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1.

You are responsible for the lawfulness of the Personal Data you provide or connect, for providing any notices and obtaining any consents or other legal bases required for its processing, and for ensuring that your instructions comply with Data Protection Laws.

4. Processing instructions

Unmanned will process Personal Data only on your documented instructions, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law, in which case Unmanned will inform you of that requirement before processing unless the law prohibits it.

The Agreement, this DPA, and your configuration of the Service constitute your complete instructions, including the assets you connect, the capabilities you enable, and the scope of testing you define under Section 3 of the Terms. Additional instructions require written agreement between the parties.

Unmanned will promptly inform you if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Laws. Unmanned is not obligated to perform a legal review of your instructions.

5. Security testing data

Authorized testing and monitoring may cause the Service to encounter Personal Data within connected systems, including identities, email addresses, credentials, session tokens, and file contents. Unmanned will treat all such information as Customer Data and as Customer Confidential Information under Section 9 of the Terms.

Unmanned will design the Service to collect only the information reasonably necessary to validate and report findings, store credentials and secrets encrypted, and use them only within the scope you authorize. Unmanned will not use credentials or access obtained through testing for any purpose other than performing the Service for you.

As stated in Section 6 of the Terms, Unmanned does not use Customer Data to train general-purpose AI models for other customers.

6. Personnel and confidentiality

Unmanned will ensure that personnel authorized to process Personal Data are subject to written confidentiality obligations or an appropriate statutory duty of confidentiality, receive appropriate training, and access Personal Data only as needed to provide, secure, and support the Service.

7. Security measures

Unmanned will implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data against Security Incidents, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing. These measures are described in Annex 2.

Unmanned may update its security measures from time to time, provided that updates do not materially decrease the overall protection of Personal Data.

You are responsible for your own use of the Service, including securing your account credentials, managing authorized users, and configuring the Service appropriately, as described in Section 2 of the Terms.

8. Subprocessors

You authorize Unmanned to engage Subprocessors to process Personal Data. Our current Subprocessors are listed in Annex 3.

Unmanned will impose data protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective than those in this DPA, and remains responsible for each Subprocessor's performance of those obligations.

Unmanned will provide at least 30 days' notice before a new Subprocessor begins processing Personal Data, by email to the account owner or security contact you designate. You may object on reasonable data protection grounds within that period. The parties will discuss your objection in good faith. If Unmanned cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, you may terminate the affected portion of the Service and receive a refund of any prepaid fees for the remainder of the subscription term for that portion.

9. Data subject requests

Taking into account the nature of processing, Unmanned will provide reasonable assistance, through the Service or otherwise, to help you respond to requests from data subjects to exercise their rights under Data Protection Laws.

If Unmanned receives such a request directly, it will not respond except to direct the data subject to you, unless required by law, and will promptly forward the request to you where the requester identifies you.

10. Security Incidents

Unmanned will notify you without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a Security Incident. The notice will describe, to the extent known, the nature of the Security Incident, the categories and approximate volume of data subjects and records affected, likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it. Where information is not available at once, Unmanned will provide it in phases as it becomes available.

Unmanned will take reasonable steps to contain, investigate, and mitigate the effects of the Security Incident and will provide reasonable cooperation to help you meet your notification obligations under Data Protection Laws.

Notification will be sent to the account owner or security contact you designate. Unmanned's notification of or response to a Security Incident is not an acknowledgement of fault or liability.

11. Impact assessments and consultation

Unmanned will provide reasonable assistance with data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with supervisory authorities that you are required to conduct under Data Protection Laws, to the extent relating to your use of the Service and where you do not otherwise have access to the relevant information.

Unmanned will first make available its standard documentation, including this DPA, its security documentation, and its Subprocessor list. Assistance beyond that documentation may be subject to reasonable fees agreed in advance.

12. Audits

Upon written request, and no more than once in any 12-month period unless required by a supervisory authority or following a Security Incident, Unmanned will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. This includes completed security questionnaires, summaries of security policies, and any third-party audit reports or certifications Unmanned maintains.

If that information is not sufficient to demonstrate compliance under Data Protection Laws, you may conduct an audit, on at least 30 days' notice, during normal business hours, at your expense, and under a mutually agreed scope designed to avoid disruption to Unmanned's operations and protect the confidentiality of other customers' data. Audit information is Unmanned's Confidential Information.

Audits do not include probing, scanning, or testing the Service, which remains subject to Section 4 of the Terms.

13. International transfers

Unmanned is located in the United States and may process Personal Data in the United States and other countries where Unmanned or its Subprocessors operate. Unmanned will ensure that transfers of Personal Data are made in compliance with Data Protection Laws.

EU transfers

To the extent Personal Data subject to the GDPR is transferred to Unmanned in a country that has not received an adequacy decision, the SCCs are incorporated into this DPA by reference and apply as follows:

  • Module Two (controller to processor) applies where you are a controller, and Module Three (processor to processor) applies where you are a processor.
  • You are the data exporter and Unmanned is the data importer.
  • The optional docking clause in Clause 7 applies.
  • In Clause 9, Option 2 (general written authorization) applies, with the notice period set out in Section 8 of this DPA.
  • The optional language in Clause 11 does not apply.
  • In Clause 13, the competent supervisory authority is determined in accordance with that Clause.
  • In Clause 17, Option 1 applies, and the SCCs are governed by the laws of Ireland.
  • In Clause 18(b), disputes are resolved by the courts of Ireland.
  • Annexes I and II of the SCCs are completed by Annexes 1 and 2 of this DPA, and Annex III by Annex 3.

UK transfers

To the extent Personal Data subject to the UK GDPR is transferred, the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Commission Standard Contractual Clauses issued by the UK Information Commissioner ("UK Addendum") is incorporated by reference. Tables 1 through 3 of the UK Addendum are completed with the information in Section 13 and the Annexes of this DPA, and in Table 4, either party may end the UK Addendum as set out in its Section 19.

Swiss transfers

To the extent Personal Data subject to the FADP is transferred, the SCCs apply as described above, with references to the GDPR read as references to the FADP, the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner as the competent supervisory authority, and the term "member state" interpreted so that data subjects in Switzerland may bring claims in their place of habitual residence.

Alternative mechanisms

If Unmanned adopts an alternative transfer mechanism recognized under Data Protection Laws, including certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions, that mechanism will apply instead of the SCCs to the extent it covers the relevant transfer. If a transfer mechanism is invalidated, the parties will cooperate in good faith to implement a valid alternative.

Government requests

If Unmanned receives a request from a public authority for access to Personal Data, Unmanned will, unless legally prohibited, notify you promptly and give you an opportunity to seek a protective order. Unmanned will challenge requests it reasonably considers unlawful and will disclose only the minimum Personal Data required to comply.

14. U.S. state privacy laws

To the extent the CCPA or similar U.S. state laws apply, Unmanned acts as your service provider or processor and will:

  • Process Personal Data only for the business purposes described in the Agreement and Annex 1.
  • Not sell or share Personal Data, as those terms are defined under the CCPA.
  • Not retain, use, or disclose Personal Data outside the direct business relationship with you or for any purpose other than providing the Service, except as permitted by law.
  • Not combine Personal Data with personal information received from other sources, except as permitted by law.
  • Comply with applicable obligations and provide the same level of privacy protection required of you.
  • Notify you if Unmanned determines it can no longer meet its obligations, and permit you to take reasonable steps to stop and remediate unauthorized processing.

15. Deletion and return

Upon termination of the Agreement, you may request an export of available Customer Data within 30 days, as described in Section 11 of the Terms. After that period, Unmanned will delete Personal Data in its possession within 60 days, unless retention is required by law. Any retained Personal Data will remain subject to this DPA and will be processed only for the purposes requiring its retention.

Personal Data in backups will be deleted on Unmanned's standard backup rotation schedule and will not be actively processed in the meantime.

16. Liability

Each party's liability arising out of or relating to this DPA is subject to Section 13 (Limitation of liability) of the Terms, except to the extent such limitation is prohibited by Data Protection Laws or by the SCCs with respect to data subjects' rights under them.

17. Term and governing law

This DPA remains in effect while Unmanned processes Personal Data on your behalf under the Agreement.

Except as required by the SCCs or Data Protection Laws, this DPA is governed by the laws and jurisdiction set out in Section 15 (Governing law) of the Terms.

Unmanned may update this DPA to reflect changes in Data Protection Laws or the Service. Updates will not materially decrease the protection of Personal Data during your then-current subscription term without your agreement.
