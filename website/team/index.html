About Unmanned

The people behind your defense.

Unmanned brings together offensive-security experts, engineers, analysts and designers to make security more proactive and easier to live with. We build VEX and run the operations behind it.

Founder & CEO

David Hunt

David has worked on both sides of security: how attackers get in, and how defenders find out. He was an offensive security contractor for U.S. intelligence agencies, worked on threat intelligence at Mandiant, led development of MITRE’s CALDERA and co-founded Prelude. Along the way, one question kept coming up: what do we actually know is working? Unmanned is his answer.

He wrote the book on it, too:  Irreducibly Complex Systems

Making security simple takes different minds.

  • Head of security

    John Jackman

    John has spent 20 years in offensive security. He led a DoD team building computer network exploitation tools, then found zero-days and wrote exploits at REDLattice. At Unmanned, he puts defenders on equal footing with attackers, with tools that are powerful and easy to use.

  • Head engineer

    Phil Urquhart

    Phil has spent nearly 20 years in software engineering, including offensive and defensive security work at Mandiant, JPMorgan and Peraton Labs. He led delivery of an advanced network defense system for the DoD. At Unmanned, he builds VEX and helps customers connect it to their environments.

  • Security engineer

    Caleb Pennington

    Caleb works in VEX every day, running security operations and IT support for Unmanned’s customers. That makes him its most hands-on user: he sees firsthand what customers need and where the product can improve. At Unmanned, he connects those building VEX with the people relying on it.

  • Head of UX

    Chad Carr

    Chad has spent more than 20 years leading UX at Best Buy, Star Tribune and Thomson Reuters, turning complex problems into clear, usable products. At Unmanned, he shapes how people understand and use VEX, from a business owner reading a Slack message to a security team digging into an investigation.

Backed by people who know what it takes.

  • Senior advisor

    Mark Petrinovic

    Mark has spent three decades in corporate banking and risk management at J.P. Morgan, HSBC and MUFG. He managed multi-billion-dollar risk positions and served on business, credit and risk committees. At Unmanned, he helps connect technical security findings with the business decisions executives and boards need to make.

  • Partner & investor

    The General Partnership (TheGP)

    Unmanned is backed by TheGP, a firm that pairs investment with hands-on company-building support. Its team brings experience across engineering, hiring and bringing products to market.
