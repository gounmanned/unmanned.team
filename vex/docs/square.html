Square

This guide walks you through locating your Square personal access token and registering it with Vex. Vex will periodically query Square for security-relevant events including team member changes, disputes, card activity, and bank account changes.

Prerequisites

  • You have access to your Square account and can log into the Square Developer Console.

Step 1: Open the Developer Console

  1. Go to developer.squareup.com and sign in.
  2. In the top navigation, click Applications and select your existing application, or click "Create your first application" if you don't have one yet.
  3. Set the application name to Vex Security Monitor and click "Save".

Step 2: Copy Your Personal Access Token

  1. On your application's detail page, click Credentials in the left sidebar.
  2. Make sure the environment toggle at the top is set to Production.
  3. Under Personal Access Token, click "Show" and copy the token.
Note: This is a personal access token tied to your Square account. Treat it like a password — do not share it or commit it to version control.

Step 3: Save to Vex

Inside Vex, add your credentials in the following JSON format:

{"token": "$TOKEN"}

Replace the token with your actual access token from the previous step.

Once registered, Vex will periodically check your Square account for security events, including new team members, disputes, card changes, and bank account activity.
