What VEX detects.

Every alert type VEX can raise from the services it connects to, listed by vendor, and what VEX does when it sees one. Search for a threat, a service or an alert ID.

Every detection shows the response VEX applies when it sees it.

  • R0LogLogged to your audit history. No further action.
  • R1LogoutSigns the account out.
  • R2ContainLocks the asset out and keeps it from getting back in.
  • R3InvestigateStarts an automatic investigation into the root cause.
  • R4RecommendWrites a specific security recommendation.
  • R5AssessRuns an offensive assessment, the way a red team would.
