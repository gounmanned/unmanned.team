Ramp

This guide walks you through creating a Ramp developer app, copying your OAuth 2.0 credentials, and storing them so Sam can monitor your account for security issues. Sam will periodically check for new or modified cards and alert you to anything suspicious.

Prerequisites

  • You have access to your Ramp account with Admin or Business Owner permissions.
  • Each Ramp card should be named after a customer domain, such as example.com, so Sam can associate activity with the correct account.

Step 1: Access the Developer Console

  1. Log into your Ramp Dashboard.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Developer in the left sidebar.
  3. Under My apps, click "Create new app".

Step 2: Configure Your App

  1. In the Create new app modal, set the App name to Sam Security Monitor.
  2. Click "Create app".
  3. On your new app's detail page, locate the Grant types section.
  4. Click "Add new grant type" and select Client Credentials.
  5. Under Scopes, click "Configure allowed scopes" and enable the following:
cards:read
  1. Click "Save" to apply the scope configuration.

Step 3: Copy Your Credentials

  1. On your app detail page, locate the Client ID and Client Secret fields.
  2. Important: Copy both values immediately — the Client Secret will not be shown again after you navigate away.
  3. Store both values securely before proceeding.

Step 4: Save to Sam

Inside Sam, add your credentials in the following JSON format:

{"client_id": "$CLIENT_ID", "client_secret": "$CLIENT_SECRET"}

Once registered, Sam will run periodic security checks on your Ramp account, including monitoring for new cards, card setting changes, and cards issued to unknown users.
